# Summary

Reconciliation engines obtain authoritative input, render or interpret it, manage assigned targets and report outcomes. Argo CD and Flux provide these responsibilities through different resource and component models. Source references, reconciliation objects and lifecycle controls need interpretation under the selected engine. Similar feature names do not guarantee identical pruning, health or ordering behavior. Ownership and supported interfaces matter more than choosing a tool solely by its dashboard.
