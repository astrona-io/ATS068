# Summary

State-store tooling should provide identified desired versions, controlled retrieval and useful audit history. Git and artifact stores can satisfy parts of this contract through different mechanisms. Mutable selectors need resolved content identity, and retained references need available content. Verification can establish provenance under a chosen trust policy but not universal safety. Agents and reviewers should know exactly which content was obtained and why it was accepted.
